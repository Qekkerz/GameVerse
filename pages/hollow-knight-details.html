Hollow Knight is a 2D action-adventure game created by Team Cherry. The journey takes place in Hallownest, an underground kingdom filled with forgotten settlements, mysterious creatures and challenging encounters.

01 / THE INHABITANTS CHARACTER INDEX

WHO YOU MAY MEET

Characters

Travelers, protectors and curious souls whose stories are woven into Hallownest.

PLAYABLE CHARACTER 01

The Knight

A quiet, mysterious wanderer who enters the ruins of Hallownest and begins uncovering the kingdom's secrets.

Read character profile

The Knight explores the underground kingdom, meets its inhabitants and discovers the history behind the kingdom's decline. Its journey is shaped by exploration and discovery.

PROTECTOR 02

Hornet

A fast and determined warrior who protects important secrets connected to the kingdom.

Read character profile

Hornet is known for her agility, needle and thread. She tests the Knight during their journey and has a significant connection to Hallownest's past.

EXPLORER 03

Quirrel

An observant traveler who visits forgotten places and searches for answers about the kingdom.

Read character profile

Quirrel is a thoughtful explorer. His journey brings him to several important locations, where his curiosity helps reveal pieces of Hallownest's history.

02 / THE CHALLENGES ENCOUNTER INDEX

TEST YOUR SKILL

Notable Bosses

Memorable encounters that challenge the Knight throughout the underground kingdom.

BOSS ENCOUNTER 01

Mantis Lords

Respected warriors who defend their territory with speed, precision and coordinated attacks.

Read encounter notes

The Mantis Lords are found in Mantis Village. Their encounter rewards careful observation, patience and learning the rhythm of each attack.

BOSS ENCOUNTER 02

Soul Master

A powerful figure found in the City of Tears, connected to the study of Soul.

Read encounter notes

Soul Master is encountered at the Soul Sanctum. The fight combines movement, timing and learning to respond to magical attacks.

BOSS ENCOUNTER 03

Hornet Sentinel

A later encounter with Hornet, taking place in the Kingdom's Edge.

Read encounter notes

This encounter builds on the earlier meeting with Hornet. The arena and faster pace make positioning and timing especially important.

03 / THE KINGDOM LOCATION INDEX

PLACES OF HALLOWNEST

Locations

Every area has its own atmosphere, history and secrets to discover.

SETTLEMENT 01

Dirtmouth

A quiet town above the kingdom, serving as a starting point for the journey below.

Discover this location

Dirtmouth sits above the ruins of Hallownest. Its small community and the well leading underground make it an important place for travelers.

WILDERNESS 02

Greenpath

A lush, overgrown region filled with plant life, pools and hidden passages.

Discover this location

Greenpath contrasts with many stone-built areas of Hallownest. Its vegetation, acidic pools and unusual inhabitants create a distinct atmosphere.

ANCIENT CITY 03

City of Tears

A vast, rain-soaked city whose architecture reflects the lost grandeur of Hallownest.

Discover this location

The City of Tears is one of the kingdom's major areas. Its constant rain, tall buildings and interconnected routes reveal much about its past.
