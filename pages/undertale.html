GAME OVERVIEW

Welcome Underground

Undertale is an independent role-playing game created by Toby Fox. The player explores an underground world filled with monsters and unusual characters. The decisions made during the adventure can change the outcome of the story.

Developer Toby Fox
Genre RPG
Release 2015
Platform PC Console

A WORLD BELOW

The Story

Long ago, humans and monsters lived together. After a great conflict, monsters were sealed underground behind a magical barrier. Many years later, a human child falls into this hidden world and begins an unusual journey. Along the way, the player meets different monsters and makes decisions that influence the adventure.
